What a kill switch actually does

A kill switch blocks all traffic the moment your VPN connection drops, rather than quietly falling back to your unprotected connection. We tested this by force-killing the VPN process mid-download.

Standing note

Free VPN apps are rarely free

Several free VPN apps we've looked at monetize through logging or reselling connection data. If there's no clear company behind a tool and no independent audit, we treat it as unverified.
